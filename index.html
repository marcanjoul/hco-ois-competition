Competition Rules
🎯 The Competition

Compete against other players to log the most OIS and earn the best sales-per-hour ratio. Track progress in real-time on the leaderboard!

📋 Adding OIS

After each shift, log your sales amount and hours worked. You can only log once per day, see a manager to edit your order if needed.

📊 The Metrics
  • Total Sales: Sum of all orders logged
  • Hours Worked: Total hours for the competition
  • $/HR: Sales divided by hours
🏅 Winning

The competition ends on the specified date. The player with the highest $/hr ratio wins!
