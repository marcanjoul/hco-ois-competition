How It Works 🏆
🎯 The Competition

Compete with your team to log the most sales and earn the best sales-per-hour ratio. Track your progress in real-time on the leaderboard.

📋 Logging Orders

After each shift, log your sales amount and hours worked. You can edit your entry for today, or add entries for past days (managers only).

📊 The Metrics
  • Total Sales: Sum of all orders logged
  • Hours Worked: Total hours for the competition
  • $/HR: Sales divided by hours (efficiency)
🏅 Winning

The competition ends on the specified date. The employee with the highest $/hr ratio wins (or total sales, depending on competition settings).
